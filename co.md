XNESS README / BUSINESS CONCEPT
What is Xness?

Xness is a virtual business plaza where businesses rent digital shops inside a giant online building called Xness Plaza.

Instead of sellers being hidden behind products, each seller gets a permanent shop location.

Example:

Xness Plaza
Floor 2
Shop 122
Nile Electronics


Customers explore the building, enter shops, interact with businesses, and buy products just as they would in a real shopping plaza.

Give every business a digital address.
